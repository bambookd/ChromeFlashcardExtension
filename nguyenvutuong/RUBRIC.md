# 1. Internship Content

Describe your work process and the tasks you performed during the internship period.

# 2. Summary

At the end of the internship, describe:

- The products, deliverables, skills, or knowledge you gained.
- Your personal reflections:
  - About the company.
  - About the internship program.

> **Note:** Students must write the report using a formal academic/report-writing style.

## Report Evaluation Criteria

The report will be assessed based on the following criteria:

- Does the internship work match the internship program registered by the company and approved by the faculty?
- Is the internship related to the student's major?
- Report writing quality (grammar, spelling, formatting, writing style, presentation, etc.).
- Did the student achieve the expected products, knowledge, and professional skills from the internship?
  - Workload completed.
  - Knowledge acquired.
  - Technical/professional skills developed.
- Other factors (if applicable).
- Final score (10-point scale).

## Detailed Grading Rubric

| Criterion | Points | Description |
|-----------|:------:|-------------|
| 1 | **2 pts** | Report is clearly presented and follows the required report format/template. |
| 2 | **3 pts** | Includes all required information about the internship company. |
| 3 | **2 pts** | Clearly describes the internship process and the tasks assigned during the internship. |
| 4 | **2 pts** | Clearly explains the knowledge and skills learned during the internship. |
| 5 | **1 pt** | Presents the products, deliverables, or outcomes achieved during the internship. |
