# Internship Worklog

### Overall

A week-by-week record of the 9-week engineering internship (June 15 – August 14, 2026). Each week details **what was done**, **key focus areas and milestones achieved**, and **concrete output artifacts** produced during the engineering of the **Chrome Flashcard Extension & Serverless Study Platform** (`chrome-flashcard-axiza`).

The internship timeline officially starts on **15/06/2026**, when the team was formed. Although the project repository was created on **22/06/2026**, weeks are counted from the team formation date.
