During my internship at First Cloud AI Journey Bootcamp from June 15 to August 16, I had the opportunity to work in a professional environment focused on cloud computing and AWS services. Throughout the internship, I gained hands-on experience with AWS services such as Amazon EC2, Amazon S3, AWS Lambda, Amazon RDS, Amazon CloudWatch, IAM, VPC, and more. In addition, I strengthened my skills in cloud architecture, scripting, troubleshooting, system monitoring, DevOps practices, technical documentation, and team collaboration.

Beyond technical development, I consistently demonstrated professionalism by completing assigned tasks on time, adhering to organizational policies and security best practices, communicating effectively with mentors and teammates, and actively seeking feedback to improve my performance. The internship also enhanced my problem-solving abilities, adaptability, and understanding of industry-standard workflows in cloud service environments.
